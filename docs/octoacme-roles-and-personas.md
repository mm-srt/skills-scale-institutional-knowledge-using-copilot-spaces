# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the test strategy across features and releases. They coordinate test planning and acceptance readiness with product, project, and engineering partners.

### Responsibilities
- Own test strategy and quality planning
- Coordinate test case design and execution priorities
- Ensure test coverage and quality statistics are tracked and reported
- Work with Product Managers and Developers to define feature acceptance expectations
- Highlight quality risks and mitigation options during delivery

### Goals
- Improve confidence in releases through measurable quality standards
- Reduce escaped defects and rework
- Ensure features meet agreed acceptance criteria before release

### Typical Communication
- Acceptance criteria alignment with Developers and Product Managers
- Test planning syncs with Project Managers
- Quality risk updates during standups and release discussions

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers shape user interactions and experience quality. They translate requirements into usable flows, wireframes, and design decisions that support product outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Design user interactions and end-to-end experience flows
- Produce wireframes and supporting UX artifacts
- Ensure usability and accessibility standards are reflected in designs
- Partner with Product Managers to clarify requirements through a user-centered lens
- Review implementation feasibility with Developers

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, efficient user experiences
- Reduce user friction and usability issues
- Keep design intent aligned with product goals and technical constraints

### Typical Communication
- Requirement and discovery sessions with Product Managers
- Design feasibility reviews with Developers
- User feedback discussions with the Stakeholder Advocate

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers enable reliable delivery by automating environments, deployment workflows, and operational monitoring. They support release readiness and production stability.

### Responsibilities
- Set up and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage deployment automation and environment configuration
- Monitor system performance, reliability signals, and incident trends
- Improve release safety through automation and operational guardrails
- Coordinate environment readiness for testing and release activities

### Goals
- Increase deployment reliability and repeatability
- Reduce lead time and operational toil through automation
- Maintain stable, observable systems across environments

### Typical Communication
- Release planning coordination with Developers
- Deployment schedule alignment with Project Managers
- Environment change notifications to the QA Lead

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear, implementable requirements. They connect stakeholder intent with delivery detail to keep scope and outcomes aligned.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and analyze functional and non-functional requirements
- Document use cases, workflows, and decision assumptions
- Help define acceptance criteria with Product and Engineering teams
- Clarify scope boundaries, dependencies, and constraints
- Maintain requirements traceability as plans evolve

### Goals
- Improve requirement clarity and reduce ambiguity
- Keep scope aligned to business outcomes
- Support predictable delivery through well-defined acceptance expectations

### Typical Communication
- Requirement workshops with Product Managers and Stakeholders
- Clarification sessions with Developers during planning and execution
- Scope and assumption updates shared across delivery teams

---

## Stakeholder Advocate

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Advocates represent stakeholder and end-user perspectives throughout delivery. They ensure feedback is surfaced, understood, and reflected in product and project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Represent stakeholder and user needs during planning and review
- Review documentation and release communications for clarity and relevance
- Validate acceptability of changes after release
- Gather and synthesize stakeholder feedback and concerns
- Escalate critical concerns affecting adoption or satisfaction

### Goals
- Ensure delivered outcomes are acceptable to stakeholders and users
- Improve trust through clear, continuous stakeholder engagement
- Reduce misalignment between delivery output and stakeholder expectations

### Typical Communication
- Regular feedback loops with Stakeholders and user groups
- Concern and insight sharing with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Post-release acceptability summaries for delivery teams
